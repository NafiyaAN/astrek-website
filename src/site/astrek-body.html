FAQ

FAQs

Common questions about Unik Exo, clinical use, and how we work with partners.

Who is Unik Exo designed for?

Unik Exo supports individuals with lower-limb immobility under clinician supervision, including stroke rehabilitation, gait training, and structured therapy programs.

How does Unik Exo ensure safety?

Safety is engineered into the system with emergency stop, motor-locked safety modes, mechanical hard limits, and step-wise therapist control throughout every session.

Can clinics pilot Unik Exo?

Yes. We partner with rehabilitation centers for structured pilots. Contact our team to discuss clinical partnership and onboarding.

What modes does Unik Exo support?

Sit, Stand, Walk, and Exercise modes, so therapists can guide patients through every stage of mobility training with one system.
